Pricing questions

Is the runtime free?

Yes. NobodyWho is open source under EUPL-1.2 and does not meter prompts, responses, or tokens.

Are models included?

NobodyWho runs compatible models. Model files and licenses come from their respective publishers.

What does deployment support cost?

It depends on the environment, hardware, and scope. Contact the team to discuss the rollout without locking in a package too early.

Can we evaluate it before a larger rollout?

Yes. Start with the open-source runtime, then bring the team into hardware, model, and deployment planning if needed.
