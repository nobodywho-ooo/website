AI infrastructure with fewer dependencies.

NobodyWho puts open models inside the product. Teams keep control of the data, hardware, and deployment.

"We want useful AI to keep working when the cloud is unavailable, unsuitable, or simply unnecessary."

NobodyWho started as a local inference engine for Godot. It now supports product teams across Python, Kotlin, Swift, React Native, Expo, Flutter, and Godot.
