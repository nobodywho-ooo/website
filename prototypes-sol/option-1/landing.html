Three lines to local inference.

Install the library, point it at a model, ask a question. The model runs where your software runs.

quickstart.py
from nobodywho import Chat

chat = Chat('huggingface:NobodyWho/
Qwen_Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF/
Qwen_Qwen3-0.6B-Q4_K_M.gguf')

response = chat.ask('Is water wet?')
print(response.completed())
