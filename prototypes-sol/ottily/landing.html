About us

Local inference, built in Europe.

NobodyWho builds on-device and on-premises inference infrastructure, so organisations can run open models without their data leaving their own walls.

Start with the SDKTalk to us

Control and simplicity, not just compliance.

Running powerful models on your own infrastructure should be straightforward, not a research project. NobodyWho keeps the system understandable while your data stays under your control.

01 / EU-native

Built close to the rules

Made and operated in the EU, with local deployment as the default architecture.

02 / Open

Open by default

The core engine is EUPL-1.2. Inspect it, self-host it, and contribute to it.

03 / Control

No lock-in

Deploy on-device, on-premises, or at the edge. The infrastructure stays yours.

04 / Cost

No per-token costs

Run inference on hardware you own and replace variable API bills with a forecastable budget.
