Pricing questions

Is NobodyWho free to use?

Yes. NobodyWho is open source under EUPL-1.2 and has no usage fee.

Are models included?

NobodyWho runs compatible models. Model files and their licenses come from their respective publishers.

Will I receive a token bill?

No. NobodyWho does not meter prompts, responses, or generated tokens.

What costs should I expect?

Your costs depend on the hardware, models, distribution, and operations chosen for your product.
