About us

Local AI, built in Europe

NobodyWho builds on-device and on-premises inference infrastructure that allows you to run large language models locally. Our clients can then  run AI whilst maintaining full control of their data and costs. 

BASED IN COPENHAGEN, DENMARK

Local AI as the default

We believe companies should have continuous access to intelligence without losing control of their data or costs.

I. EU-native

Built and operated in the EU from day one, aligned with GDPR and the EU AI Act by design.

II. Open at our core

The core engine is open source under EUPL 1.2. Inspect it, self-host it, contribute to it.

III. Control, not lock-in

Deploy on-device, on-prem, or at the edge. The infrastructure, and the data, stay yours.

IV. No per-token costs

Inference runs on server and hardware you already own. That gives you a fixed, forecastable budget line.

Frequently asked questions

What is NobodyWho?

NobodyWho is an open-source engine for running large language models on-device or on-premises, built by a Copenhagen-based team for European companies that want AI without sending data outside their own infrastructure.

Is NobodyWho open source?

Yes. The core engine is open source under the EUPL 1.2 licence and available on GitHub.

Does the EU AI Act require on-premises deployment?

No. The EU AI Act does not legally mandate on-premises deployment. NobodyWho recommends it for control and simplicity, not as a compliance requirement.

Where is NobodyWho based?

NobodyWho is based in Copenhagen, Denmark, and operates within the EU.
